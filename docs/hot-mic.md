# Hot Mic

[Open Hot Mic](https://garellano.github.io/type-racer/#game=hot-mic) from the game chooser or its direct link. The host chooses **2–9 attendees**, shares their screen, and presses **Start passing**. Teammates do not need a link, an account, or any interaction. The microphone passes automatically for 13 seconds, accelerates, makes two false pauses, and finally lands on the opening speaker.

## Selection and recovery

The setup lists the nine explicitly requested team presets. Each person can use any existing character. Choices freeze during the round. A cryptographic draw gives every checked attendee an equal chance before any animation starts. The subsequent handoffs are presentation: their speed, seat position, browser performance, and sound cannot change the outcome.

The browser saves setup preferences locally and the complete round in the current tab's session storage. Saved data is validated before use. Refreshing or returning from a hidden tab shows the same fixed draw at its current position, including the final result if the round has already finished. A round expires two hours after its creation. Storage restrictions still allow an in-memory game, but refreshing may lose its saved state.

This is a host-operated screen-sharing game, without a server-verified result or independent participant screens. A host can start another draw, and manually clearing or modifying browser data is outside the casual fairness guarantee. The existing multiplayer games keep their server-authoritative rules.

## Motion and sound

The initial motion setting follows the browser preference. **Reduce motion** replaces flying arcs and character reactions with discrete holder changes. Hidden pages and completed rounds stop animation work. The page releases its listeners, scheduling, and audio when leaving the game.

Sound starts off. Use **Sound off** to enable passing notes, office applause and cheers, and a short finish fanfare. Muting or hiding the page stops active and scheduled effects. Returning to an already finished round does not replay its celebration. The notes and fanfare are generated by Web Audio. The office samples reuse the existing [audio sources and CC0 licensing](derby-audio.md).

The seated illustrations reuse the approved Office Derby artwork, preserving both legs in front and the established team likenesses. No new photo references are published. See [artwork provenance](derby-art.md). The game chooser's small microphone symbol is an original vector illustration.

## Validation

Run `npm run check` for TypeScript, formatting, rule and audio lifecycle tests, the existing Worker tests, and both build checks. Browser verification covers opening through the chooser and direct link, a complete round, attendee selection, sound activation, reduced motion, and recovering the fixed result after refresh.
